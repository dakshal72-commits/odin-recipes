Chicken Taco Salad

Back to Home
  • 1 lb chicken breast, cooked and shredded
  • 1 head of romaine lettuce, chopped
  • 1 cup cherry tomatoes, halved
  • 1 cup black beans, drained and rinsed
  • 1 cup corn kernels
  • 1/2 cup shredded cheddar cheese
  • 1/4 cup sliced black olives
  • 1/4 cup diced red onion
  • 1/4 cup chopped fresh cilantro
  • Taco seasoning to taste
  • Sour cream and salsa for serving
  1. In a large bowl, combine the shredded chicken with taco seasoning and mix well.
  2. In a separate large salad bowl, layer the chopped romaine lettuce, cherry tomatoes, black beans, corn, shredded cheddar cheese, black olives, red onion, and fresh cilantro.
  3. Add the seasoned shredded chicken on top of the salad.
  4. Serve with sour cream and salsa on the side.
