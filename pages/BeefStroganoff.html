Beef Stroganoff

Back to Home
  • 1 lb beef sirloin, cut into strips
  • 1 cup sour cream
  • 2 tbsp butter
  • 1 onion, diced
  • 2 cloves garlic, minced
  • 1 cup beef broth
  • 1 tbsp flour
  • Salt and pepper to taste
  1. In a large skillet, melt the butter and sauté the onion and garlic until fragrant.
  2. Add the beef strips and cook until browned.
  3. Stir in the flour and cook for 1 minute.
  4. Pour in the beef broth and bring to a boil.
  5. Reduce heat and simmer for 20 minutes or until the beef is tender.
  6. Stir in the sour cream and season with salt and pepper.
