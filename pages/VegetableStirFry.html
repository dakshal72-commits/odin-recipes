Vegetable Stir Fry

Back to Home
  • 2 cups mixed vegetables (bell peppers, broccoli, snap peas)
  • 1 cup tofu, cubed
  • 2 tbsp vegetable oil
  • 3 cloves garlic, minced
  • 1 tbsp ginger, grated
  • 3 tbsp soy sauce
  • 1 tbsp honey
  • 1 tsp sesame oil
  1. Heat the vegetable oil in a large skillet or wok over high heat.
  2. Add the garlic and ginger and cook for 30 seconds.
  3. Add the mixed vegetables and stir-fry for 3-4 minutes until crisp-tender.
  4. Add the tofu and cook for another 2 minutes.
  5. In a small bowl, whisk together the soy sauce, honey, and sesame oil.
  6. Pour the sauce over the vegetables and tofu, tossing to coat evenly.
